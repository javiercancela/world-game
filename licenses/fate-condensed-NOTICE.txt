Adaptation: fate-condensed-cli-v1 changes the skill list and allocation, replaces
Physique with Athletics for physical stress, and supplies three authored stunts.
It uses deterministic NPC spending, a shared GM scene pool, no NPC invokes on
static checks, event compels only, finite harm descriptions and concession losses,
and explicit computer-game scene/session boundaries and consequence recovery.
